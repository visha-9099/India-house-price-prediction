🏡 India House Price Prediction
This repository provides a comprehensive analysis and machine learning model for predicting house prices in India based 
on various factors such as location, size, amenities, and economic conditions. The project aims to help homebuyers, real estate 
Investors, and property developers make data-driven decisions.

📌 Features
📊 Real Estate Data & Market Trends
Location-Specific Pricing: House prices across major cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, etc.) and suburban areas.
Property Types: Apartments, independent houses, villas, plots, and commercial properties.
Size & Configuration: Price variations based on BHK (1BHK, 2BHK, 3BHK, etc.), square footage, and floor levels.
Property Age & Condition: Impact of new vs. resale properties on pricing.
🏙 Geographical & Economic Influences
Neighborhood Trends: Analysis of prime locations, emerging real estate hubs, and price fluctuations.
Connectivity & Infrastructure: Influence of metro stations, airports, business districts, IT hubs on housing prices.
Economic Indicators: Correlation between house prices and factors like interest rates, inflation, and GDP growth.
